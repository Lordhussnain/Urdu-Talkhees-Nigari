4-Step Talkhees Formula

Follow this repeatable four-stage sequence before writing your final answer on the examination sheet.

Step 01

Read Carefully (2–3×)

اصل عبارت کا غائر مطالعہ کریں

Read the passage multiple times to grasp the author's central idea (مرکزی خیال) and underline essential phrases with a pencil.

Step 02

Draft Rough Key Points

اہم نکات کو الگ نوٹ کریں

Strip away examples, similes, quotes, and repetition. Jot the remaining core ideas in rough work before composing prose.

Step 03

Write 1/3rd in Own Words

تہائی حصے میں اپنے الفاظ لکھیں

Connect your points into a single cohesive paragraph at roughly one-third of the original word count without copying lines verbatim.

Step 04

Assign a Concise Title

اوپر ایک مناسب عنوان دیں

Place a brief, meaningful title (مجوزہ عنوان) above the précis that captures the unified theme of the entire passage.

Six Core Rules of Talkhees Nigari

Every solved passage in the Compendium adheres strictly to these textbook and tutorial guidelines.

1. The One-Third Length Rule

ایک تہائی اصول

A 90-word passage should condense to roughly 30 words, and a 150-word passage to ~50 words. Preserve every core idea without exceeding the proportional budget.

2. Meaningful Title Selection

مناسب عنوان کا انتخاب

Choose a crisp 2–6 word title reflecting the passage's central message rather than a minor detail. Board examiners allocate dedicated marks to the title.

3. Original Expression

اپنے الفاظ کا استعمال

Synthesize ideas in clear, natural Urdu instead of lifting sentences directly from the original text. Keep the prose unified in a single paragraph.

4. Prune Unnecessary Extras

غیر ضروری تفصیلات کا اخراج

Remove illustrative anecdotes, similes (تشبیہات), stacked synonyms, poetry quotes, and repetitive clauses while retaining historical facts essential to the argument.

5. Indirect & Objective Voice

ذاتی تبصرے سے گریز

Write in direct third-person narrative tone. Never insert personal opinions or meta-commentary such as "مصنف کے مطابق" or "شاعر کہتا ہے".

6. Accurate Punctuation

رموزِ اوقاف کا درست استعمال

Use Urdu commas (،), semicolons (؛), and full stops (۔) accurately so complex condensed clauses remain effortless to read.
